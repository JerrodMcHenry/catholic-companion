**Counts:** quotation marks across all 185 topics were counted by automated pattern matching, then remaining candidates were reviewed by hand. Counts are approximate. Links to external sites are references, not reproduction. Explanations written for the app are original text.

## Remaining permissions actions before public release

1. Confirm the app will be **free and non-commercial**; this is the condition of ICEL's website permission.
2. Confirm with ICEL that its online publication policy is current.
3. Ask LEV whether short, attributed quotations of Vatican documents in a free educational app need any permission.
4. Have a reviewer spot-check that no verbatim NABRE text remains, and review the policies for the other traditions' sources.
