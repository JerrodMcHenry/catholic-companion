# Review Checklist (closeout, 2026-11-25)

All content in Catholic Companion was researched and written with AI assistance and checked against sources. **None of it has had independent theological, pastoral, or legal review.** This checklist gathers what remains before public release. Details are in `docs/content-log.md`, `docs/content-depth-inventory.md`, `docs/spiritual-warfare-research.md`, and `docs/permissions-inventory.md`.

## 1. Independent theological review (qualified theologian or priest)

- [ ] Scripture paraphrases (2026-11-25 permissions pass): every former NABRE quotation is now an explanation in our own words; check that each accurately represents its passage.
- [ ] Holy Orders: who may be ordained (Ordinatio Sacerdotalis), the common versus ministerial priesthood, and the character.
- [ ] Original sin: "contracted, not committed," the justice objection, and the line between doctrine and open questions about human origins.
- [ ] Inerrancy: the presentation of the two readings of Dei Verbum 11, and Providentissimus Deus.
- [ ] Sexuality: principles, chastity, and pastoral tone; same-sex attraction wording (CCC 2357–2359).
- [ ] Problem of evil and the homepage answer on suffering.
- [ ] Salvation history: the distinction between explicit New Testament typology and patristic readings.
- [ ] The seven homepage answers ("Life's Greatest Questions").
- [ ] The new truth topic: the philosophical arguments against relativism, and the distinction between philosophy and revealed doctrine.
- [ ] Spiritual Warfare: temptation versus consent; whether demons can read thoughts (presented as Aquinas's opinion); the angelic hierarchies (presented as tradition); how the terms oppression, obsession, and infestation are framed; scapular and medal promises; exorcism and the 1985 letter.
- [ ] Mass as sacrifice, transubstantiation, the necessity of Baptism, canon 915, and grave matter and culpability (carried forward from earlier phases).

## 2. Pastoral review (experienced priest, OCIA director, or counselor)

- [ ] Church scandals section: harm acknowledged first, reporting routes (U.S. and elsewhere).
- [ ] Family opposition to conversion (`telling-family`).
- [ ] Doubt and spiritual dryness; ongoing struggle with sin; scrupulosity guidance.
- [ ] Intrusive thoughts and OCD guidance (`temptation`); mental-illness safeguards (`exorcism-and-deliverance`).
- [ ] Occult practices: guidance for people who took part.
- [ ] Funerals and grief, including the 988 crisis line and the CCC 2283 wording on suicide.
- [ ] Anointing of the Sick: guidance for families.
- [ ] Annulment and previously married OCIA participants.

## 3. Historical verification

- [ ] Claims resting on the Catholic Encyclopedia (1907–1912): Theophilus as the earliest surviving use of "Trinity," Corpus Christi (1264), Benediction, and the Miraculous Medal.
- [ ] Traditional dates: 1 Clement (~96), Ignatius (~110), Didache (late first century).
- [ ] Pending items from earlier phases, such as the Testimonium Flavianum and printed-Missal rubrics.
