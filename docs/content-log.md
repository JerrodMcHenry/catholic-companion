# Content Verification and Review Log

One entry per topic. **Status values:** *Draft* (researched, written, and verified against sources) → *Reviewed* (signed off by a named external theological reviewer) → *Published*.

**Important:** content is researched and drafted with AI assistance and verified against the sources listed. No topic below has received independent theological review. Nothing in this log or in the app may describe a topic as approved until a named reviewer signs it off here.

## How verification was done (Batch 1)

- **Catechism:** every paragraph number was located on the official Vatican page the app links to, and the paragraph text was read; an automated check confirmed that all 56 Catechism links in the content point to the page that holds each cited paragraph.
- **Scripture:** every cited chapter was opened on bible.usccb.org (NABRE), and every quoted phrase was matched word for word.
- **Church documents:** every document URL was opened and its title confirmed; quoted phrases (Dei Verbum 10, Lumen Gentium 25, Fidei Depositum, the 1998 Profession of Faith) were matched in the official text.
- **Historical sources:** St. Ignatius of Antioch, *To the Smyrnaeans* 8 (New Advent); Tacitus, *Annals* 15.44 (Church and Brodribb translation, Livius); Josephus, *Antiquities* 20.9.1 (Whiston translation, Perseus).
- **Resources:** every URL returned the expected page; title, author, and publisher confirmed (Catholic Answers pages by page metadata; YouTube videos by the official oEmbed title and channel). Third-party re-uploads were rejected.
